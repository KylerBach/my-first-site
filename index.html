Introduction to AI

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that aims to create software or machines that exhibit human-like intelligence. This can include learning from experience, understanding natural language, and solving problems.

Recent Developments in AI

In recent years, AI has made significant strides in various domains, including natural language processing, computer vision, and reinforcement learning. Some notable advancements include the development of large language models like GPT-3, which can generate human-like text, and breakthroughs in image recognition and autonomous systems.
